Wanderlane is a fictional brand and this site takes no bookings, so there is very
little to say here — but the document exists because a real travel site needs one,
and because it demonstrates where the boundary between "copy" and "legal text"
should sit.

## What we collect

One analytics cookie, and only after you accept it. Nothing else. There is no
account system, no payment processing and no mailing list.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect names, addresses, payment details or passport information,
because there is nothing on this site that would require them.

## Where the data goes

Analytics events, if you accept them, go to a single processor and are retained
for twelve months.

## Your rights

You may withdraw consent at any time by clearing the cookie for this domain.
