These terms describe how a Wanderlane booking would work if Wanderlane were a real
company. It is not, and nothing on this site is bookable.

## Booking and deposits

A 20% deposit confirms a booking. The balance falls due 14 days before arrival.

## Cancellation

Cancel more than 14 days before arrival and the deposit is refunded in full.
Inside 14 days we refund whatever we manage to re-let.

## The property

Minimum stay is two nights, three over a public holiday. City tourist tax is
collected at the property.
